How the sleeve works

1
Build the noise area

For each half-hour slot, average SPY's absolute move from the open over the prior 14 sessions. Bands are anchored on today's open and yesterday's close.

2
Trade the break

At 10:00, 10:30, … 15:30 ET, go long only when price is above the upper band. One trade per day.

3
Trail and flatten

The stop is the higher of the upper band and VWAP, checked at each half-hour decision point. Everything is flat by 15:45 ET.
